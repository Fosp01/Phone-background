# iPhone cleanup plan

The goal is one clean Home Screen, a few folders, and everything else in the App Library.

## 1. Delete what you don't use (5 min)
- **Settings › General › iPhone Storage**: open each app you haven't used in months and tap **Delete App**.
- Turn on **Offload Unused Apps** on the same screen. It frees space but keeps your data.

## 2. Make new apps skip the Home Screen
**Settings › Home Screen & App Library › Newly Downloaded Apps › App Library Only.**
This stops the clutter from coming back.

## 3. Hide extra pages
Long-press an empty spot › tap the page dots at the bottom › untick every page except the first.
Hidden apps stay available in the **App Library** (swipe left past your last page).

## 4. Build the folders
Long-press an icon › **Edit Home Screen** › drag one app onto another to make a folder. Rename it with short names.

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Social** | Messages-style apps: WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, Facebook, X, Discord |
| **Media** | Music, Spotify, YouTube, Netflix, Podcasts, Photos editors |
| **Work** | Mail, Calendar, Notes, Files, Office/Google apps, Slack, Teams |
| **Money** | Bank apps, Wallet, PayPal, shopping (Amazon etc.) |
| **Travel** | Maps, Uber, airline and train apps, Weather |
| **Health** | Health, Fitness, sleep and food apps |
| **Utilities** | Settings, Calculator, Clock, Camera tools, VPN, Authenticator |
| **Games** | All games |

## 5. Suggested layout (pairs well with the dark wallpaper)
- **Dock (4):** Phone, Messages, Safari, Camera
- **Top row:** a Clock or Weather widget, or leave it empty so the light streak shows
- **Middle:** the 8 folders above in two rows
- **Bottom row:** your 4 most-used single apps

## 6. Match the dark look
- **Settings › Display & Brightness › Dark**
- Long-press Home Screen › **Edit › Customize › Dark** to give the icons the dark style.
- **Settings › Wallpaper › Add New Wallpaper › Photos** › choose `wallpaper-graphite.png`. Use it for both screens and turn **off** Legibility Blur and Depth Effect to keep it crisp.
